Prompt
Create a hypertext narrative that guides web visitors through a fictional or non-fictional story, a journey through a topic you're interested in, or some sort of poetic text that unfolds over several pages. (What is a hypertext narrative? Review last week's "Hypertext Narrative - Proposal" assignment description for a detailed explanation and artist examples.)

As a reminder, your hypertext narrative should consist of at least five HTML pages linked together by hyperlinks.

I want your main focus to be on developing the story and interaction of your project. Your hypertext narrative could unfold in a linear fashion (page 1 leads to page 2 leads to page 3, etc.) or a non-linear fashion (page 1 leads to page 3 or 5, page 5 leads to page 2, page 2 leads to page 4 or 3, etc).

Note: Do not use this exercise to create an online portfolio.

All the HTML pages that make up your final hypertext narrative should live in a single folder called "hypertext" within the GitHub repository you made for this class.
